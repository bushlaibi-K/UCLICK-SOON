Questions

Before you visit

Do I need to book?

We recommend booking ahead, especially for weekends, occasions and bridal. Call your branch to ask about same-day availability.

How much do services cost?

Prices depend on the service and your hair length or look. We confirm the price with you when you book, so there are no surprises.

How early should brides book?

As early as you can, so we can plan your trial and keep your date free for you.

Is the salon for ladies only?

Yes. Khawla Beauty is a ladies' salon at both branches.
